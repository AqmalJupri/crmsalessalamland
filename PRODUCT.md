# Salam CRM Product Register

## Product

Salam CRM is the shared operational CRM for Salam Land, Bumi Hayat Printing, and Barakah Emas. The CRM surface is used by marketing and sales teams; Tasha is the Salam Land sales-administration surface.

## Primary users

- Marketers and sales staff who qualify leads, manage follow-ups, and monitor pipeline activity.
- Sales administrators who manage Salam Land inventory, orders, finance, tasks, and reports.
- Managers who need scoped, auditable operational metrics across authorised companies.

## Product purpose

Give each user one secure, company-scoped place to see current records, take authorised actions, and understand the source, period, and freshness of operational metrics. The interface must never invent data, access, session health, or cross-company authority.

## Brand personality

Calm, precise, dependable, and direct. Product copy uses concise Malay for orientation, status, safeguards, and actions.

## Current reference lock

- Context: a compact operational workspace used on desktop and mobile, often in bright office or field conditions.
- Direction: light, restrained, data-led, and familiar; the approved Tasya-inspired direction is interpreted through the existing production token contract because the reference host was unavailable during review.
- Colour roles: navy for brand/navigation, neutral white and slate for working surfaces, blue for actions and selection, amber only for attention, and semantic green/red for outcome states.
- Type: Inter with compact UI sizes, tabular numerals for operational values, and no decorative display treatment.
- Structure: one visible page title, direct controls, bounded tables, divider rows on small screens, and cards only for self-contained interactive or metric units.
- Interaction states: keyboard-visible focus, explicit destructive confirmation, field-level validation, truthful loading/empty/error states, and reduced-motion support.

## Anti-references

- Decorative gradients, emoji icons, indigo or violet theme defaults.
- Motivational product copy, redundant subtitles, decorative notes, or fabricated status text.
- Card-within-card layouts, generic card wrappers around tables, or one card per mobile table row.
- Hidden company scope, client-derived authority, or cross-business record leakage.

## Accessibility baseline

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard operation, visible focus, labelled controls and data tables, focus-managed dialogs and menus, 320px reflow, 200% zoom, reduced motion, and status meaning that does not depend on colour alone.
